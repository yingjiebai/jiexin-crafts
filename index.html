Product ranges

Four ranges of rustic metal, iron, resin and MgO decor for home, garden and Christmas.

Rusty Metal Christmas Decorations Wholesale

Rust-finish metal stars, star lanterns, reindeer, Christmas tree silhouettes, snowmen and Santa figures for indoor and outdoor Christmas decor. We also make hanging ornaments and candle holders, all in a corten-look rust finish.

Wrought Iron Garden Decor Supplier

Metal garden stakes, birds and animal sculptures, birdhouses, planters and plant stands. Our wrought iron wall art and garden ornaments suit garden centres and home decor retailers.

Cast Iron Garden Ornaments & Hardware

Cast iron door knockers, boot scrapers, hooks and animal statues. Antique-style hardware in a vintage finish, cast in our own factory.

Resin & MgO Garden Statues

Hand-painted polyresin animals and gnomes for gardens and patios. MgO (magnesium oxide) planters, lanterns and concrete-look garden statues for home and garden decor.

Seasonal decor for Christmas, Easter and Halloween, plus OEM/ODM private label: your designs, sizes, colours and packaging.

30–100 pcs per design, and you can mix different items in one container.

Yes. Send a sample, photo or drawing; we develop it to your design, size, colour and packaging.

What are your price terms and shipping port?

FOB Xiamen in USD; our price list shows HS code, net weight and carton size for each item.

When should I order Christmas decorations?

Place Christmas orders by March–April for the coming season.
